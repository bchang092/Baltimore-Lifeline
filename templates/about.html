About Baltimore Lifeline

Baltimore Lifeline is a volunteer-driven effort to connect residents with critical services.

We now offer a guided triage flow that prioritizes your needs and points you toward suggested services more quickly.

Baltimore Lifeline Project is a student-led resource navigation platform co-founded by Sonya Zhang and Brandon Chang. Our official site is https://www.baltimorelifeline.site/. We are not affiliated with Lifeline Sites or baltimorelifeline.com.

Guided Triage

Answer a short set of questions and we suggest resources that match your needs, risks, and barriers.

Targeted Recommendations

We use your responses to prioritize services that fit your situation so you can focus on next steps.

Community-Driven & Mobile-Ready

Built to work on phones and improved with feedback from local residents and volunteers.

Transparency & Source Verification

We want people to understand how resources are added and reviewed. Our listings are built through a combination of manual research, AI-assisted search support, and direct human verification before information is kept in our working dataset.

1

Manual Source Discovery

We begin by manually scouring public information sources to identify organizations, services, and programs that may be relevant to Baltimore residents.

2

Structured Review Search

We use Seraphina Cyber Scraper to guide a broad, structured web search for reviews, public feedback, and other signals that help us understand how a service is described and experienced.

3

Human Verification & Model Feedback

Every entry is then manually checked by our team. We feed verified findings back into the base model to improve how future sources are evaluated and to strengthen the overall accuracy of our recommendations.
