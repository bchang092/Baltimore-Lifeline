About the project

About Baltimore Lifeline

Baltimore Lifeline Project is a student-led resource navigation platform co-founded by Brandon Chang and Sonya Zhang.

We built the platform to help residents find health, food, legal, and other support services with less friction and more clarity.

Our official site is https://www.baltimorelifeline.site/. We are not affiliated with Lifeline Sites or baltimorelifeline.com.

What the platform does

Baltimore Lifeline combines fast discovery, guided triage, and community feedback so residents can move from uncertainty to actionable next steps.

How it helps

  • Find resources fasterBrowse the map or use guided help to narrow the field quickly.
  • Prioritize what mattersWe focus on relevance, accessibility, and reducing dead ends.
  • Keep improving the dataCommunity feedback and human review shape the dataset over time.
  • Community-Driven & Mobile-ReadyBuilt to work on phones and improved with feedback from local residents and volunteers.
Reliability note

Important: our reliability descriptions are based on what we found across calls, forums, online reviews, public listings, and similar sources at the time of review, with AI used to help review and verify source patterns. Services can change, staff can change, and experiences can vary. If you find information that differs from what is shown here, treat our summary as the predominant pattern we found rather than a guarantee of current conditions.
