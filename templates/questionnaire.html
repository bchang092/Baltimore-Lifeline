Get guided help

This short questionnaire helps prioritize what you need first, then matches you to the most relevant resources in the current dataset.

If you are in immediate danger, seriously injured, or cannot stay safe right now, call 911 immediately. If you are in a mental health or substance-use crisis, call or text 988.
If you skip a question, we treat it as the least urgent or no-additional-need option. Unchecked multi-select questions are treated as no additional needs.
