About Us

Baltimore Lifeline is a volunteer-driven effort to connect residents with critical services. Our interactive map highlights clinics, food resources, and legal aid—curated for clarity and speed.

We believe access should be simple. If you have suggestions or updates, we’d love to hear from you.

Reliability note: our reliability descriptions are based on what we found across calls, forums, online reviews, public listings, and similar sources at the time of review, with AI used to help review and verify source patterns. Services can change, staff can change, and experiences can vary. If you find information that differs from what is shown here, treat our summary as the predominant pattern we found rather than a guarantee of current conditions.

Transparency: How We Verify Sources

We want people to understand how resources are identified and reviewed. Our process combines manual research, AI-assisted search support, and direct human verification before information is retained in our dataset.

1

Manual Source Discovery

We manually scour public information sources to identify organizations, services, and programs that may be relevant to Baltimore residents.

2

Structured Review Search

We use Seraphina Cyber Scraper to guide a broad, structured web search for reviews, public feedback, and other signals that help us understand how a service is described and experienced.

3

Human Verification & Model Feedback

Each entry is manually checked by our team, and verified findings are fed back into the base model to improve how future sources are evaluated.
